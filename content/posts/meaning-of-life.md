Whats the Meaning of Life?
But FIrst of all, whats the meaning of that question?
What is meaning? Its Significance, right?
The meaning of a word is what it signifies.
Whats the meaning of a banana? The word "banana" just represents the object banana.
But does that object banana means anything?
It has a meaning because we eat it.
If it were not edible then it would have a differnet meaning.
maybe it would be something that is significant to Botanists.
Then its not just a non-edible banana - it would not be a banana as we mean it!
What I am trying to say is that A banana has a different meaning for a Farmer, Botanist or a common man.
Because it signifies a different thing to them even if it is objectively a Banana.

Similarly what we have to ask here is, What is the signifance of Life itself.
That doesnt make any sense unless we ask the question "to whom?".
What is the significance of my Life to you, my family, friends and me?

For most people My life is meaningless cause they dont know that I this perticular person does exist.
That changes entirely when it comes to people who know and care about my existence.
Especially me, what does my life signify to myself?
What do I give importance in my life?
People I love, Hobbies, Job or something else too?
Maybe Beauty - Of nature, of Intelligence and elegance...
Of objects or people or anything. Different for different people.
For some people, at some particular point in life, There is nothing of significance.
It might be temporary or last indefinitely.
At that point they can say - my life has no meaning.

But the existence of meaning doesnt say whether we should live or not.
Cause someone can have a lot of meaning like they are significant to their family or society or even to their future self even if they doesnt feel that way right now.
That doesnt compel them to keep living.
similarly lack of meaning doesnt compell one to end their life either.

So what is the meaning of my life?
Nothing right now.
